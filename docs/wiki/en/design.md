# Hotbar design

How your tool slots are marked in the hotbar: **frame**, **corners**, **underline**, **background** or a **texture** from your resource pack - plus color, opacity, thickness and spacing.

Open it via:
- the button in `/toolbar`,
- its own key (under **Options → Controls → MRtoolbar**, not bound by default),
- "Configure" in Mod Menu.

It is saved on your PC in `config/mrtoolbar.json` - so the design applies on every server with MRtoolbar.

## For resource pack creators
The style "Texture" draws `assets/mrtoolbar/textures/gui/sprites/hud/tool_slot.png` (20×20, nine-slice with a 3 px border). It is tinted with the chosen color - white keeps the original colors.
